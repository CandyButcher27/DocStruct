Datasets and selectable text

Clearly yes: DocLayNet, DocBank, PubLayNet, PubTables-1M, SciTSR, READOC-arXiv. These are labeled and digital-born, and the PDF text is selectable or derived from embedded source text.

Likely yes, but verify release format: OmniDocBench, READOC-GitHub, READOC-Zenodo, TableBank, FinTabNet, TabLeX family.

Usually not ideal for selectable-text PDF parser benchmarking: image-only or OCR-heavy datasets, and scanned-document corpora.
